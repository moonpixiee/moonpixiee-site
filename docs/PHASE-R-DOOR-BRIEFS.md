## 1. Room Door Design Template (revised, reusable)

Every door is specified with:

1. **Name** — displayed label (presentation layer)
2. **Route** — existing route it opens (from repo; frozen)
3. **Hall status** — Primary / Secondary standalone
4. **Reference** — assigned mood reference, if any (never copied)
5. **Mood** — 2–4 sentences: what standing before this door feels like
6. **House grammar (constants)** — how this door expresses: framed opening · architectural surround · visible threshold · leading path/line · one controlled boundary-crossing sign of life
7. **Signature hue** — the one saturated identity color
8. **Primary material** — the door's physical voice
9. **Ornament density** — sparse / measured / rich
10. **Light** — where it comes from, what it promises about the interior
11. **Room leak** — the ONE primary trace escaping into the Hall
12. **Label treatment** — how the text label sits with the door (always present, always legible)
13. **States** — default / hover-focus / activation (300–500 ms)
14. **Reduced-motion variant** — static equivalent of every state
15. **Mobile presentation** — appearance in the vertical procession

A door with no ratified brief does not enter the Hall.

## 2. The Seven Doors

### 2.1 Luna's Room — PRIMARY, **DEFERRED FROM PHASE R HALL** (RQ-7 ratified)

- **Route:** none — the room is unbuilt. `/becoming` and `/letters` are ratified as NOT Luna's Room. This brief remains ratified and on file; the door enters the Hall as a **House event** when Luna's Room is built and published.
- **Reference:** R3 (pink filigree Parisian) — personality and ornament only, not literal color
- **Mood:** Someone extraordinary lives here, and they chose to let you knock. Private without being closed; expressive without performing. The most personal door in the Hall, and it knows it.
- **House grammar:** arched opening in pale architecture; surround kept quiet so the door itself carries the voice; threshold swept and cared-for; a short direct approach — this door is near, not distant.
- **Signature hue:** translated from R3's energy through the ratified directions — House Red, softened rose, dark wood, blackened metal, warm ivory, or another approved expression. **Not bubblegum pink.**
- **Primary material:** to propose in visual phase (candidates: lacquered wood with fine metal filigree)
- **Ornament density:** rich — Luna's Room may be more visually expressive than the other rooms. Privacy does not require dullness.
- **Light:** warm, low, personal — lamplight rather than architecture light.
- **Room leak (ratified):** handwriting
- **Label:** "Luna's Room"
- **Mobile:** full-bleed presence; ornament legible at small scale or simplified, never smeared.

### 2.2 Library — PRIMARY

- **Route:** `/library` (`src/pages/library.html`) — verified repo + production, 28 Jul 2026
- **Reference:** none assigned — direction from words; reference gap acknowledged.
- **Mood:** Ornate, aged, scholarly, warm, quietly grand. A door that has been opened many thousands of times and expects to be opened many thousands more. It does not raise its voice.
- **House grammar:** tall framed opening; stone surround with age visible; worn threshold — the wear IS the ornament; approach lined by the suggestion of shelves beyond.
- **Signature hue:** deep warm neutrals — aged oak, leather, brass (to propose).
- **Primary material:** old dense wood, brass hardware.
- **Ornament density:** measured — grandeur through age and proportion, not decoration.
- **Light:** warm interior light through a gap or transom: reading light, late.
- **Room leak (ratified):** one loose page
- **Label:** "Library"
- **Mobile:** height emphasized; the door should still feel tall in a vertical stack.

### 2.3 The Edit — PRIMARY

- **Route:** `/edit` (`src/pages/edit.html`) — verified repo + production, 28 Jul 2026
- **Reference:** R2 (burgundy Parisian, gilt ornament)
- **Mood:** Polish, fashion, ceremony, controlled richness. The door equivalent of being well-dressed on purpose. Nothing accidental survives here.
- **House grammar:** formal arch; carved surround at its most composed; immaculate threshold; a direct, presented approach.
- **Signature hue:** burgundy / lacquer.
- **Primary material:** lacquered wood, gilt-bronze ornament.
- **Ornament density:** rich, but disciplined — richness under control is the point.
- **Light:** reflected — gilt catching light rather than emitting it.
- **Room leak (ratified):** fabric edge
- **Label:** "The Edit"
- **Mobile:** gilt detail must survive compression; if it can't, simplify the ornament rather than blur it.

### 2.4 Moss & Moon — PRIMARY, **DEFERRED FROM PHASE R HALL** (RQ-7 ratified)

### 2.5 Machine Room — PRIMARY

- **Route:** `/tools` (`src/pages/tools.html`) — verified repo + production, 28 Jul 2026. Label-only change per RQ-2; the M3 rename to `/machine-room` is not live and must not be targeted.
- **Reference:** R6 (matte-black pedimented doors) — structural reference only.
- **Direction (ratified):** **Victorian-industrial, not generic industrial.** An impossible modern mechanism installed beneath old architecture. Never warehouse, spaceship, or cyberpunk nightclub. It still belongs to the same House.
- **Mood:** Severe, dark, minimal ornament, deliberately distinct — the room where the House's machinery lives, built by someone who read too much and slept too little.
- **House grammar:** rectilinear frame under a Victorian pediment — the one door where the arch may flatten; blackened architectural surround; precise hard threshold; approach marked by a change in floor material.
- **Signature hue:** matte black; one restrained mechanical accent (to propose).
- **Primary material:** matte-painted wood or steel, machined details.
- **Ornament density:** sparse — precision as ornament.
- **Light:** low mechanical glow from beneath or within seams.
- **Room leak (ratified):** low mechanical glow
- **Label:** "Machine Room"
- **Not Yet vocabulary:** if Machine Room access is genuinely unavailable at any point, the ratified "Not Yet" treatment (R7) may be used here — provided it never implies a functioning action that does not exist.
- **Mobile:** severity reads instantly at small scale; this door benefits most from restraint.

### 2.6 VELVET//CTRL — PRIMARY, **DEFERRED FROM PHASE R HALL** (RQ-7 ratified)

- **Route:** none — the eyebrow "Velvet//Ctrl Presents" on `/tools` is ratified as not a destination. This brief remains ratified and on file; the door enters the Hall as a **House event** when VELVET//CTRL is built and published.
- **Reference:** R5 (black-and-red ornate) — atmosphere only. **No skull or skeletal ornament.** Magnetic and advanced, never horror-themed.

- **Primary material:** dark carved wood or composite, red inlay or backlight.
- **Ornament density:** rich, abstracted.
- **Light:** red — emitted, controlled, slightly impossible.
- **Room leak (ratified):** impossible digital shimmer
- **Label:** "VELVET//CTRL"
- **Mobile:** the red-on-black contrast is the mobile advantage; keep the shimmer subtle enough to survive reduced-motion as a static gradient.
