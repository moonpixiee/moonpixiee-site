## The Doctrine

Every screen answers one question before any layout question:

> **What room am I standing inside?**

The House does not have pages with backgrounds. It has rooms with walls. The UI is the actor; the set is why anyone remembers the scene. The enemy is named: **AI polish** — the centered card, the perfect gradient, the floating panel, the tasteful anonymous arch. Beautiful, forgettable, identical. The House should not look designed. It should look **inhabited** — as though people have been quietly living there for decades.

## The Two Completions

**LAW 1, completed — the lighting plot.** Every screen declares its light source(s) before styling begins: a lamp out of frame, moonlight through an opening, a fireplace, glowing machinery. One key light per scene. Ambient, sourceless glow is prohibited — if a glow cannot answer "where is this coming from?", it doesn't render. Light *pools* and *falls*; it does not float.

**LAW 2, completed — the walls.** Darkness is constructed, never void. The threshold's arch is carved **into** a wall, not placed on black: wall plane in front, light and depth within the opening. Dark surfaces carry tooth — plaster, stone, velvet — built from value shifts and grain, so they read as touchable.

## The Constraint Annex (non-negotiable — the set passes the same gates as everything else)

1. **Budget:** the set is built from craft, not kilobytes — layered CSS gradients as motivated light, low-opacity grain for tooth, value construction for walls. Photographic wall textures are a last resort and live inside the ratified budgets (hero ≤250KB, LCP <2.5s). The brief demands the feeling and forbids the file size.
2. **Legibility outranks atmosphere:** text zones sit on quiet surfaces; texture lives where words don't. Contrast is computed against the *actual local surface* beneath the text, not the nominal token. Whispering may reduce emphasis, never legibility.
3. **The still must hold:** the room exists fully at 380px and in `prefers-reduced-motion`. Atmosphere may breathe (LAW 6), but the settled, motionless frame is a complete room, not a degraded one.
4. **The protection clause stands:** set design adds atmosphere, never copy. No explanation appears beneath the ratified lines.

## Inspiration Reorientation (ratified direction, pending signature)

UI inspiration collection stops. The reference diet becomes: architecture · film stills · museums · libraries · chapels · galleries · conservatories · tea houses · boutique hotels · stage lighting · editorial photography — and above all, **Moonpixiee's own visual language**: the forgotten conservatory at midnight. The House competes with places people remember, not with websites.

## Scene Brief — The Threshold (production-designer format)

> **INT. THE THRESHOLD — NIGHT.**
>
> One room. A single archway, carved into a charcoal-plaster wall that catches moonlight along its upper reaches. The wall is real: it has weight, tooth, and a faint memory of hands.
>
> One lamp burns somewhere out of frame, low and warm — its light pools on the threshold floor and brushes the inner edge of the arch. Through the opening: depth, not darkness. The suggestion of a hall beyond, lit from within.
>
> The words are not floating on a page. They are *in the room* — the wordmark inscribed above or within the arch (the Architect engraves; it does not hover), the greeting lit by the lamp's spill, the invitation resting in the quieter light below.
>
> Garnet appears exactly once: where the House invites someone forward. **Wander** sits like a seal pressed into the scene — warm, small, certain.
>
> Nothing on this set explains itself. The visitor should feel like the last guest in an old hotel, the first visitor to a museum after closing, someone stepping into a conservatory just after rain — somewhere they have never been, and somehow miss.
>
> Emotional target, unchanged and governing: *"…I've never been here before… but it feels like this place has been waiting for me."*

**Technical margins for the scene (from the annex):** wall from value construction + ≤2 gradient layers + grain overlay ≤3% opacity; lamplight as radial gradient pools with a consistent key direction; arch interior one step lighter than wall face; text zones on the quietest surfaces; all of it holding as a still at 380px.
