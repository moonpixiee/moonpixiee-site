## The Ratified Lines

```
The House has been expecting you.

Wander if you like

                 Wander
```

- **"The House has been expecting you."** — full stop. The sentence is complete; the House is speaking. (Narrator voice.)
- **"Wander if you like"** — no terminal period, ratified deliberately: the invitation hangs open, and the thought completes in the act of clicking.
- **"Wander"** — the entire button. One quiet verb. No arrow, no icon, no "Enter," no second word. Permission instead of instruction.

Voice notes for the record: the greeting claims *expectation, not knowledge* — fully compatible with the pre-identity boundary (RQ-4: browser convenience, not House memory; §3 memory begins with keys). The button is invitation-class and therefore speaks in House Red per LAW 3's corollary — the first Garnet a visitor ever sees.

## The Protection Clause (ratified)

These lines are protected fiercely:

- No A/B testing.
- No optimization.
- No SEO language added.
- No explanation of the House beneath them.

If someone doesn't understand them immediately, that's okay. The Threshold isn't trying to convince people to stay. It's simply opening the door.

**The governing distinction (ratified):** future contributors do not ask *"Will this convert better?"* They ask *"Does this still sound like the House?"* The clause protects the Threshold's purpose, not merely its words.

## AM-R-003 — RATIFIED (amends PHASE-R-CONCEPT-BRIEF.md §2.2)

- **Ruling A — RATIFIED.** "Wander" replaces "Enter the House" as the threshold's primary action. Rationale of record: by the time the visitor reads the button they are already at the threshold — the emotional action isn't crossing a property line, it's choosing how to move through it. Wander grants permission, carries no productivity baggage, implies curiosity over destination, invites exploration without promising completion. "Enter the House" retires with honor into design history — it served its purpose; the House found a quieter voice. (Historic uses in the preserved specimen boards remain untouched, per the design-history rule.)
- **Ruling B — RATIFIED: relocate, do not retire.** *"A home for becoming."* leaves the threshold — two competing introductions would crowd a door that whispers — and becomes a sentence visitors **discover rather than are told**: candidate homes include browser metadata, the footer, the House Map, the About space, documentation, repository branding, a future printed manifesto. Specific placement is decided during shell and House Map work, by eye and in context.
