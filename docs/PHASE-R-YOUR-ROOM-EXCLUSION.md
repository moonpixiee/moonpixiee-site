# PHASE-R-YOUR-ROOM-EXCLUSION.md

**Ratified: 28 July 2026 (RQ-3)**

## Standing

**Your Room is not implemented and not displayed during Phase R.** No door, no locked state, no "Not Yet" placeholder, no visual reference to it anywhere in the Threshold, Grand Hall, House Map, or any room.

## Rationale

Your Room is too important to introduce as a decorative promise before identity exists. Its first appearance belongs to the identity phase, where it arrives as an actual event:

> A door that did not exist before now exists because the House knows who has returned.

The reveal is stronger than a standing promise.

## Scope of the "Not Yet" vocabulary

The "Not Yet" visual language (reference R7) is **reserved**, not discarded. It may be used only for a real, existing destination whose current state is genuinely unavailable (e.g., Machine Room access), and must never imply a functioning action that does not exist. It is never used for Your Room during Phase R.

## Enforcement

- Phase R acceptance testing includes a check that no Your Room element exists in any state, viewport, or navigation surface.
- Any Your Room concept work produced during Phase R is logged as an identity-phase proposal, never rendered.
