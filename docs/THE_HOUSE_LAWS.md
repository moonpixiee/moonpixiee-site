# 🌙 The House Laws
### House of Luna · the rules that sit above every design decision

> Once something becomes a **Law**, neither we nor any future contributor gets to casually change it. A Law is not a preference or a color choice — it is philosophy. Design decisions answer to the Laws; the Laws answer to nothing but a deliberate, ratified amendment. *(Ratified by Luna, 15 Aug 2026.)*

*The Constitution governs how the House **behaves**. The House Laws govern what the House **is** — its time, its light, its darkness, and the one thing it exists to create.*

---

# ⚖️ THE PRIME LAW

## LAW 0 — Build Memories, Not Features

Every room, every object, every sound, every interaction, every piece of light must answer **one question**:

> ### "Could this become someone's memory?"

If yes, it belongs. If no, it probably doesn't. This is **The Memory Test**, and it is the highest law in the House — every other Law and every manuscript serves it.

**The north star is not** *beautiful*, *immersive*, or *cozy.* **It is *memorable.*** We are not doing UX. We are doing **Memory Design** — building things a person might carry with them for years. The rarest thing a place can do is make someone nostalgic for it *while they are still inside it.* That is the whole ambition of House of Luna.

**Feature thinking → Memory thinking:**
- ❌ "Add rain." → ✅ *"I remember sitting by the window while the rain slowly raced down the old glass."*
- ❌ "Add books." → ✅ *"I wasn't looking for that book… somehow it found me."*
- ❌ "Play music." → ✅ *"I still listen to that fireplace playlist when I can't sleep."*
- ❌ "Friend system." → ✅ *"I met one of my closest friends because I knocked on the wrong door."*

**The consequence for how we build:** every room leaves behind **one memory**, not one feature. And every manuscript and every Visual Bible layer ends with a final page — **The Memory** — a single line naming the memory we are trying to create. If we can't write that line, the thing isn't ready.

> *If someone, on a hard day, opens House of Luna, walks to the fireplace, and thinks "I'm home" — the Prime Law is fulfilled.*

# 🏛 THE ARCHITECTURAL LAWS

## LAW 001 — The House Exists Outside Time
The House does not follow the real world's clock. There is no morning, no afternoon, no sunset. **Inside the House, it is always night** — the hour when the rest of the world has finally gone to sleep. The House belongs to that hour.

## LAW 002 — The Night Is Alive
The night is never static. Clouds drift. Stars twinkle. The moon changes slowly across the month. Rain occasionally falls; snow visits in winter; a distant storm sometimes lights the far landscape. **The world beyond the windows continues whether anyone is watching or not.**

## LAW 003 — Moonlight Is Sacred
The moon is the House's oldest light. **Moonlight is cool; firelight is warm.** The contrast between the two creates the emotional atmosphere of every room. **No artificial white lighting exists** in the House — ever.

## LAW 004 — Fire Is Life
Every inhabited room contains a source of warmth: a fireplace, candles, a lantern. The House is **never brightly lit.** A visitor should always feel drawn *toward the warmth.*

## LAW 005 — Darkness Is Comfortable
Darkness is **never** used to frighten. It exists to create **intimacy.** Corners may remain unseen; not everything must be revealed. **The House rewards curiosity** — it does not punish it.

## LAW 006 — The Moon Keeps Its Own Calendar
The House follows the **real lunar cycle.** 🌑🌒🌓🌔🌕 On a new moon the windows are darker, the room quieter, the fire dominant. Two weeks later, a full moon pours silver through the glass and the shadows move differently. **No announcement. No "Full Moon Event!" popup. It simply is.** Someone who has visited for months one night thinks *"I swear this room looks different than it did last week"* — and they are right. That is how the House proves it is alive without ever saying so.

## LAW 007 — The House Never Rushes You
*(Luna christened this Law #27.)* **Nothing in the House appears immediately.** Not menus. Not quests. Not notifications. Not tutorials, popups, or "let's get started." The House **waits.** It has waited a thousand years — it can wait five more seconds for you. On arrival there is silence, and space, and the trust that you'll look around; only after you've had a moment to *arrive* does anything gently offer itself. This is an invisible decision no one will consciously notice — but everyone will feel. It is the difference between opening an app and *coming home.*

---

## LAW 008 — The House Reveals Itself Slowly
> *"Rooms shouldn't reveal themselves all at once — they should unfold gradually, rewarding curiosity."* — Luna

No space shows everything from any one vantage. You never see the whole House at once. What you *can't quite* see is the invitation to go further: a glimpse, a spill of light, a sound from the next room. Someone clicks "The Library" expecting a page and instead **begins a journey** — Door, threshold, warm light, silence, fire — and *only then* does the Library reveal itself. This makes every space feel **earned, not loaded.** A room that appears all at once is a page; a room that unfolds is a place.

## LAW 009 — Every Transition Is a Walk
You never tap a button and cut to a new screen. Moving between spaces always **feels like walking deeper into the House** — even when it takes only ~600ms: the camera moves, the light changes, and **you hear the next room before you see it.** There are no page loads in the House — only thresholds crossed. *(The zone engine already honors this — `walkTo` moves the camera toward the thing you touched, then fades through; this law makes it universal.)*

## LAW 010 — The House Has No Menu; The World Is the Interface
The House is **architectural, not linear.** You are never on rails. Every room is a **space you stand inside**, not a hallway you're pushed down — you can always wonder *"what's over there?"* and simply go. And there is **no visible menu, ever. The shelves are the menu.** You navigate by touching the world itself: books, lanterns, staircases, tables, letters, candles, doors. Objects *are* the interface.
And the magic is **curiosity, not puzzles.** Discovery is never gated by riddles or challenges — no *"solve this to enter."* The feeling is *"I wonder what happens if I click that,"* never cleverness. A shelf with a moon carved into it swings open a hidden passage; a book left sticking out rotates a secret bookcase — rewards for **attention**, never tests. *(Already the engine's grammar: invisible hotspots, `walkTo`, objects-as-doors — no menu anywhere in the House.)*

## LAW 011 — The Map Is Earned
**No map exists at first.** You genuinely wander, get a little lost, and *discover* — *"wait… there's a poetry shelf?"* That first-week disorientation is a **feature**; it is how memories are made. Then the House **remembers.** Once you've been here, the Keeper quietly offers a small map — *"You've been here before"* — not because the House needed one, but because **you earned one.** Both experiences coexist: **first visit, you walk; hundredth visit, you may jump straight to your chair by the fire.** The House keeps its mystery for the explorer *and* becomes a home the resident can move through with ease.

## LAW 012 — The House Is One Connected Place
**No room is an island.** From anywhere in the House you can *sense* its neighbours — a warm glow, a fire's crackle, rain on far green glass, music drifting from above, the smell of tea. You always **feel the next room before you enter it.** Rooms bleed into one another; the House is a **castle, not a gallery of separate images** — you can hear the kitchens, you glimpse the staircases, and the whole place feels connected. *(Extends LAW 009 from motion to stillness: even standing perfectly still, the House reaches toward you with sound, light, and scent. This is what turns a collection of beautiful rooms into one living place.)*

## How the Laws work

- **Supreme & immutable by default.** A Law overrides any design decision, art choice, or feature request that conflicts with it.
- **Amendable only by ratification** — a deliberate, named decision by Luna, never a casual change and never because a tool, a document, or a contributor suggested it.
- **They govern everything downstream** — every Room Manuscript, every Visual Bible layer, every line of code and pixel of art must be able to answer to them.

*The House bends around its Laws. Its Laws do not bend around new features.* 🖤🌙
