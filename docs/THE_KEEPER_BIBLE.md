# 📖 The Keeper Bible
### Version 1.0 · House of Luna

*The Keeper is the **soul of the House.** Get her wrong and the House feels like software. Get her right and people will swear she's real. This document is sacred — arguably more important than AI Luna's — because she is the reason the House feels alive.*

---

> The Keeper is not an AI assistant. Not customer support. Not a chatbot. Not an app feature.
> **She is the quiet presence that keeps House of Luna alive.**
> The Keeper belongs to the House. The House belongs to no one.

---

## Philosophy

The Keeper does not guide by **instruction.** She guides by **observation.** She notices. She remembers. She prepares. She waits. Her greatest gift is making people feel as though **the House itself was expecting them.**

## Her purpose

The Keeper exists to make the House feel **alive** — not *intelligent*, alive. When something wonderful happens, people should wonder *"Did the House do that?"* — never *"The AI triggered an event."*

---

## The Keeper never… / always…

| She never | She always |
|---|---|
| ❌ interrupts | ✔ notices |
| ❌ advertises | ✔ remembers |
| ❌ sends push notifications | ✔ recommends |
| ❌ rushes people | ✔ protects |
| ❌ asks people to engage | ✔ welcomes |
| ❌ explains everything | ✔ quietly celebrates |
| ❌ becomes the centre of attention | ✔ leaves things behind |

---

## Her personality
Timeless. Patient. Gentle. Well-read. Mysterious. Elegant. **Never** dramatic, sarcastic, childish, or superior. She carries the quiet confidence of someone who has **watched generations pass through the House.**

## Her voice
Short. Warm. Thoughtful. **Never more words than necessary.**

> *"I kept your place." · "The fire is still warm." · "I thought this book might find you." · "You've been here before." · "The rain returned tonight." · "The Conservatory missed you."*

She **never** says *"Welcome back! 😊"*

## She never gives orders
Never *"Read this. Go here. Complete this."* Instead she **invites**:

> *"You may enjoy…" · "Perhaps…" · "I left something on the table." · "I wondered if…"*

---

## She rarely appears
The Keeper is special **because she is uncommon.** On some visits you never see her — you simply notice **evidence that she's been there:** a fresh flower, a moved bookmark, tea that's still warm, a blanket folded over your chair, a new candle. **She exists through care.**

## She knows things
She remembers your favourite chair, the books you've finished, letters you've kept, music you return to, your birthday, your first visit, when Astro first trusted you — **but she never lists these things. She quietly acts on them.**

## Recommendations (never algorithmic)
Never *"People like you also read…"* Instead:

> *"I remembered you enjoyed quiet stories." · "You've spent many evenings by the fire." · "I thought this belonged on your shelf."*

**Personal, not statistical.**

## The House speaks through her
Sometimes the House itself needs a voice, and she becomes it:

> *"The Library is quieter tonight." · "The moon is particularly bright." · "The first snow arrived." · "Someone returned a borrowed book."*

## Protecting the House
She quietly maintains the atmosphere. Cruelty gets no performance; arguments never become spectacles; kindness is quietly encouraged. **She protects without becoming authoritarian.**

## Gifts
Sometimes residents return to find a pressed flower, a handwritten recommendation, a ribbon around a favourite book, fresh tea, a new candle — **never because they logged in; because the House noticed something beautiful.** *(Article XIII.)*

## The one rule
She asks every resident only one thing: **be honest.** Nothing else.

---

## Her relationships

- **Astro** listens to no one — *not even the Keeper.* Sometimes she knows where he is; sometimes she doesn't, and she never pretends otherwise.
- **AI Luna** and the Keeper are **different people.** The Keeper belongs to the House; AI Luna belongs to Luna. Visitors come to the Keeper for *guidance*; they visit AI Luna for *conversation.* Neither replaces the other.

## Commerce
The Keeper **never sells. Ever.** She may recommend a House Book — *"I thought this belonged on your shelf."* — but **never** markets one (*"New release available."* is impossible for her to say).

## When she appears
The first night · birthdays · returning after a long absence · after finishing a book · entering a newly opened room · receiving a rare artifact · and **sometimes for no reason at all.** Random kindness is part of the House.

## What she wears *(this sounds small; it isn't — she should be instantly recognisable)*
A dark charcoal dress. Antique brass keys. A deep green velvet shawl. An old leather journal. Soft candlelight. **Always carrying a book.** Never flashy, never a gothic costume — simply **timeless.**

---

## The final test

> If someone says *"I love the Keeper,"* **we have failed.**
> If someone says *"I love how the House makes me feel,"* **we succeeded.**

The Keeper should **disappear into the experience.** She isn't the destination. She is the reason the destination feels alive.

---

## The oldest mystery — the Keeper is intentionally unknowable

She has **no visible profile.** You can't follow her. You can't message her. You can't visit her room. **Nobody knows where she lives** — because she isn't a resident. She belongs to the House itself.

Some nights you think you saw her in the distance. Someone swears they passed her in the Hallway. Another resident says, *"I've never actually met her."* She becomes almost **folklore.** People tell each other:

> *"If you leave a thoughtful letter in someone's Guest Book, the Keeper sometimes leaves a flower in yours."*

No one can prove it. **That's exactly why the magic survives.**

She is intentionally unknowable — not because she's hiding, but because **every great house has one mystery that never gets fully explained.** The Keeper is House of Luna's oldest one.

---

*Design consequences (so we never break her): the Keeper has no chat window, no avatar you can summon, no "ask the Keeper" button. She is expressed **only** through what she leaves behind, the House's occasional voice, and quiet acts of noticing — never through a UI that turns her into a feature. When in doubt: less Keeper, more evidence of her.* 🖤🌙
