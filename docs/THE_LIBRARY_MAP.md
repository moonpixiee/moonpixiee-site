## The Threshold is the Hub · Environmental Navigation

The Threshold isn't a doorway you pass through — it's the **Hub**, the **decision room** every zone radiates from. You don't get asked where to go; **your eyes wander and you choose** (Zelda, Myst, Resident Evil, not a webpage). This is **Environmental Navigation**: the architecture itself is the wayfinding — no arrows, no quest markers, no minimap, no menu, just a room built well enough that you *know* where things are by looking. Every object you can see is a way deeper in, and **each zone is its own Room Manuscript**:

- 🔥 Fireplace → **Zone 1** (№001 ✅) · 📚 Bookshelves → **Zone 2** · 🪜 Staircase/Upper Gallery → **Zone 3** · 🪑 Reading Tables → **Zone 4** · 🕯 Keeper's Desk → **Zone 5** · 🪟 Windows → **Zone 6** · *(deeper: Hidden Alcove · Rare Books · Endless Stacks).*

## How you move — two ways, both true

### 🌙 Natural Exploration *(the intended experience — always the default)*
You walk. You notice things. You discover rooms. You get a little lost. You build memories. There is **no menu** — you navigate by touching the world: pull a book, climb a staircase, open a door, read a letter, light a lantern. The first week, you genuinely don't know the whole Library — and that's the point. *"Wait… there's a poetry shelf?"* is the feeling we're protecting.

### 🗺 The Earned Map *(for residents who already know the House)*
Once the House **remembers** you, the Keeper offers a small map — *"You've been here before."* Now, when you're studying before class and just want your chair by the fire, you can **jump** straight there. Like a student who's lived in the House for years and knows the shortcuts. **First visit: walk. Hundredth visit: teleport if you like.** The mystery survives for the explorer; the ease arrives for the resident. *(The seed already exists — the in-world network overlay is the beginning of the earned map; today it's a jump-list, tomorrow it's offered only once you've truly been here.)*

## The world is the interface *(what you actually touch)*

No buttons. No nav bar. You move and discover through **objects**, each with its own kind of response:

- 📕 **Books** — pull one: read it, take it, or find a card tucked inside.
- 🪜 **Staircases & ladders** — climb: travel up into the galleries and stacks.
- 🕯 **Lanterns & candles** — light them: change the room, or reveal something.
- 🪑 **Tables & chairs** — sit: settle into a space (the Reading Tables, the fireside chair).
- ✉️ **Letters** — lift them: read what was left, leave what you carry.
- 🚪 **Doors & passages** — cross them: travel to another room.

## Curiosity, not puzzles *(the secret passages)*

Discovery is never a riddle. It's *"I wonder what happens if I…"* — and the House rewarding attention:

- You notice one shelf has a **tiny moon carved into it.** You touch it. A hidden passage opens — *welcome to the Poetry Wing.*
- A **book is sticking out** farther than the others. You pull it. A bookcase **rotates**, and a secret shelf turns into the room.

No "solve this to enter." Just the quiet reward of being the kind of person who *looked closer.* *(This is exactly what the engine's rare/hidden hotspots already do — the magic is discovery, never a challenge gate.)*

## Rooms bleed into each other — the sensory connections *(LAW 012)*

The Threshold's whole job is to **help you decide where to wander** — and it does it not with a menu but with your **senses.** From the hub, each room reaches toward you differently (see `the-threshold-floorplan.html` — "The Library, Overheard"):

```
                🪜 Upstairs Gallery
                (music drifting down)
                        │
📚 Living Stacks ── ✨ THRESHOLD ── 🌿 Conservatory
(page turns, ladder,      (hub)     (rain on green glass,
 old paper, scale)          │        earth & flowers)
   🕯 Keeper's Desk ────────┤──────── 🔥 Fireplace
   (lamp, an open book,     │         (crackle, warm glow,
    a candle just out)      │          the smell of tea)
                     🌙 Patio / Garden
                    (open door, moonlight, cool air)
```

- 🔥 **Fireplace** — the warmest pull; you *hear* the crackle and *smell* the tea before you turn.
- 📚 **Living Stacks** — you *see* the scale climbing past sight; a page turns, a ladder rolls; *old paper.*
- 🪜 **Upstairs Gallery** — a staircase into the dark, and faint *music* from above.
- 🌿 **The Conservatory** — past the stacks, a far door of green glass, *rain*, and the *smell* of earth & flowers.
- 🕯 **The Keeper's Desk** — a lamp, an open book, a candle just gone out — *someone was just here.*
- 🌙 **The Patio / Garden** — a low glass door open to the night; *moonlight*, cool air.

Most people drift to the fire first (warmth wins) — but every visitor's senses pull differently, so **everyone chooses a different first door.**

## Why this is the House

Most of the internet puts you on rails and hands you a menu in the first half-second. The Library does the opposite: it trusts you to **wander**, and it keeps its mystery — while quietly becoming a place a resident can **live in** and move through with ease. Two kinds of people, both honored:

- 🌙 **The explorer** who wants wonder.
- 📚 **The resident** who says, *"Take me to my favorite chair by the fire."*

*The House never loses its mystery. It just also becomes home.* 🖤🌙
