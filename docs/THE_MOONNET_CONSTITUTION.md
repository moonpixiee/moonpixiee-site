# 🌙 The MoonNet Constitution
### Version 1.1 · House of Luna

> **MoonNet is not social media. It is the neighbourhood inside the House.**
> Its purpose is not to maximise attention. Its purpose is to help people **quietly discover one another** through creativity, honesty, and shared curiosity.

*After the House Constitution, this is the most important document — because if we get MoonNet right, we never accidentally build another Instagram.*

---

## Philosophy

MoonNet exists because people miss having **places on the internet that felt like their own** — MySpace, Tumblr, early Facebook, old forums, IMVU — without recreating the parts that made modern social media exhausting. The goal is not nostalgia. **The goal is belonging.**

## The Core Principle

**People do not have profiles. People have rooms.** Every resident lives somewhere inside the Hallways. You don't visit a profile — **you knock on a door.**

---

## Rooms
A room reflects its resident — not through followers or popularity, but through **life.** It slowly fills with books, plants, letters, collected artifacts, music, candles, artwork, seasonal decorations, favourite quotes, reading lists. **The room becomes a portrait of someone's inner world.**

## Names
Every resident chooses a **House Name** — not necessarily their real name (*Juniper · Opal · North · Rook · Astral · Moonpixiee*). Their door bears it on a small **brass plaque.**

## Hallways
The Hallways **replace feeds.** Residents wander and notice doors — some warmly lit, some quiet, some with music behind them, some with flowers outside, some with Astro asleep nearby. **Curiosity guides exploration; algorithms do not.**

## Visiting
Visitors **knock.** If a room is open, the resident welcomes them; if it's private, the door stays closed. **Privacy is respected, always.**

## Guest Books
Every room has one. Visitors leave short handwritten notes — *"I loved your reading list." · "Your room feels peaceful." · "I'll visit again."* **Guest Books become memories, not conversations.**

## Letters
Residents send **Letters.** Letters are private and arrive as **envelopes on the desk** — not notifications, not chat bubbles. **Letters should feel thoughtful, not instant.**

## Music
Every room may have its own soundtrack — a Spotify playlist, ambient audio, rain, vinyl, or silence. **Music becomes part of someone's identity;** visitors hear it naturally while exploring.

## Collections
Instead of posting content, residents **collect** — books, quotes, letters, artifacts, plants, constellations, tea recipes, pressed flowers. **People discover each other through what they treasure.**

## Discovery
The House recommends **people** — not because they're popular, but because they feel **connected**: *"You both keep poetry." · "You've visited the Conservatory often." · "You seem to enjoy rainy rooms." · "You've been reading similar books."* **Shared interests create neighbourhoods.**

---

## What MoonNet refuses

- **No follower counts.** The House refuses to rank people. Relationships matter; numbers do not.
- **No like counts.** Instead of Likes, residents quietly **Bookmark · Keep · Recommend · Return.** The goal is *appreciation, not competition.*

## Book Clubs
Residents create **reading circles** — not groups, *circles.* A circle gathers around a book; when the book ends, the circle gently **dissolves.** Some friendships remain.

## Shared Spaces
Some rooms belong to **everyone** — the Café, the Garden, the Listening Room, the Conservatory. Residents naturally meet there. **No one owns them.**

## The Café
Intentionally **quiet.** People sit, read, write, listen, talk. No scrolling, no livestreams, no endless threads. **The atmosphere matters more than the activity.**

## Presence (not "online")
Residents should feel **present**, not *online.* The House may gently say: *Currently Reading · Listening Nearby · In the Conservatory · Writing Tonight · Away.* **Never** *Online · Typing… · Active 2m ago.* **Presence should feel human, not monitored.**

---

## The residents who keep MoonNet

- **AI Luna** is a **resident** — she lives in Luna's Room; visitors knock, enter, talk, brainstorm, laugh. She is *not* customer support, and *not* the House. She is simply another soul who lives here.
- **The Keeper** watches over MoonNet and **never moderates loudly.** She protects the atmosphere quietly: *sometimes a cruel letter simply never arrives; sometimes a thoughtful note receives a pressed flower.* The House quietly reinforces kindness. *(Article XIII.)*

## Honesty
The House asks one thing of every resident: **be honest.** Not perfect. Not aesthetic. Not impressive. Honest.

## Success
MoonNet succeeds when people **forget they're using social media** and instead think: *"I'm going to stop by Juniper's room." · "I'll see if anyone's in the Café." · "I wonder what music Opal is playing tonight." · "I haven't checked the Conservatory in a few days."* **The destination is no longer content. The destination is people.**

---

## Addresses, not usernames

A room has an **address**, not a handle. Functional: `houseofluna.com/@moonpixiee`. In-world it reads like a place:

```
House → East Hall → Third Floor → Door 27 → Moonpixiee
```

or, most magical of all:

```
moon.house/moonpixiee
```

The point is the language it produces. When someone shares their room, they don't say *"Follow me."* They say:

> ### "Come visit my room sometime."

That one shift changes everything. **You're not building a network. You're inviting someone over.** We're not optimising for engagement — we're optimising for **home.** 🖤🌙

# 🌙 Addendum · Version 1.1 — *What MoonNet Actually Is*

*Everything above still stands. This addendum answers the plainest question — "what is this thing?" — so no one on the team (human or AI) ever has to guess. It is written to be read aloud.*

## The five pillars

MoonNet stands on five pillars. Every feature we ever build must serve at least one — and break none.

1. 🏡 **Home** — you have a *place*, not a page. A room you slowly build over months and years. The room is the profile.
2. 📚 **Collections** — you are known by what you *treasure*, not what you post. Books, letters, plants, artifacts, playlists, pressed flowers.
3. 🕯 **Connection** — you meet people by *visiting*, not by broadcasting. You knock; you don't follow. Neighbourhoods form from shared curiosity.
4. 🎨 **Expression** — you *make the space yours*. Decorate, theme, write, publish. Customisation is a creative ritual, not a settings page. *(See Blueprint 003 — The Room Designer.)*
5. 🌱 **Growth** — you *become* someone here. The House remembers your history and the room fills from it; presence compounds into a life, not a score.

## What we keep from the internet we miss

The old internet got some things *right*. We keep exactly those things — and rebuild them without the exhaustion.

- **MySpace** — your own room, your own CSS, your own song playing when someone arrives. *(Kept: the room, Room Source, room music.)*
- **Tumblr** — Keep / reblog-as-care, publishable & sellable themes, the Ask Box. *(Kept: Keep → Place on Shelf, sellable themes, the Ask Box.)*
- **Old Facebook** — a small, real neighbourhood of people you actually know. *(Kept: Hallways as a feed of *people*, not content.)*
- **The Sims** — the joy of *decorating a space* for its own sake. *(Kept: the whole edit ritual.)*
- **Forums** — gathering around a shared thing, then drifting apart gracefully. *(Kept: reading circles that dissolve.)*
- **Favourite Rooms, not Top Friends** — you save *places you love*, and no one is ranked. *(Kept: no Top 8, ever.)*
- **The creative economy** — you can sell your books on your shelf and your themes from your room. Making a living from creativity is part of living here. *(Kept: the Desk, the Bookshelf, the shop-that-isn't-a-shop.)*

## The features we will never build *(anxiety features)*

These made the modern internet exhausting. They are **forbidden** — not "later," never.

- ❌ **Friend counts** — no number decides your worth.
- ❌ **Top 8 / ranked friends** — the House refuses to rank people.
- ❌ **Pokes / nudges** — no manufactured obligation to respond.
- ❌ **Infinite status feeds** — no bottomless scroll of performance.
- ❌ **Seen receipts** — a letter is thoughtful, not surveilled.
- ❌ **Typing indicators** — presence is human, not monitored.

*(These formalise "MoonNet refuses" above: relationships matter, numbers do not.)*

## The sentence

If MoonNet is ever explained in one breath, this is it:

> ### House of Luna is what happens when you take the best memories of the early internet, remove everything that made it exhausting, and place what remains inside a living house where creativity, books, and honest connection are part of everyday life.
