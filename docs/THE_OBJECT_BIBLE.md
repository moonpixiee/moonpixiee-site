# 📖 The Object Bible
### House of Luna v1.0

*The document that makes the House feel **intentional.** After this, nothing is "just decoration." Every object exists for a reason, means something, and can grow — even if that meaning isn't implemented until years later.*

> **Objects are not decoration. Objects are stories waiting to be discovered.**

---

## Philosophy

A visitor should never wonder *"Why is that chair there?"* They should think *"I wonder if that chair does something…"* **Curiosity is part of the House.** If an object exists, it should eventually have meaning.

## 🎁 Artifacts — *memories made visible*

Every artifact tells a story. **Artifacts are never random loot** — they are memories made visible:

- A pressed flower from your first spring.
- A ribbon from finishing your first House Book.
- A feather Astro left on your windowsill.
- A bookmark from a resident you admire.
- A seashell collected during a summer event.

*(These are exactly what fill the Room Designer — see that spec: kept artifacts become placeable decor.)*

---

## The Living House Rule

Objects **slowly change over time.** Books move. Candles burn lower. Plants grow. Tea cools. Windows collect rain. Letters pile up. Rooms become lived-in. **Nothing stays perfectly frozen forever.** *(Constitution, Articles X & XI.)*

---

## The Object Test

Before adding any object, ask — does it **Teach? Comfort? Connect? Remember? Inspire? Reveal?** If the answer is *no*, it probably doesn't belong. *(Constitution, Article VI.)*

---

## 🌙 The whisper — every object has a story

Every object should eventually reveal a **story on click-and-hold** — not a tooltip. A **whisper.**

- A candle → *"The Keeper lights this one whenever someone returns after a long absence."*
- A teacup → *"Still warm. Someone was here not long ago."*
- A pressed flower → *"Left in the Conservatory by a resident who never signed their name."*
- An old book → *"This copy has been borrowed 1,247 times. Every reader left with a different favourite page."*

Suddenly the House isn't filled with UI elements. **It's filled with stories.** *(This is the deepest form of the House's existing whisper system — placards from shadow, but for meaning, not just a name.)*
