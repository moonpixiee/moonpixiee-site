# 📖 Blueprint 002 — Becoming a Resident
### The Resident Journey · House of Luna v1.0

*This document defines what it feels like to become part of the House. It is not onboarding. It is **belonging.** Every other document answers "what should this feature do?" — this one answers "why does it exist?"*

---

## Philosophy

House of Luna is not an app people use. **It is a place people slowly begin calling home.**

Every system is measured against one question:

> **"Would this make someone want to come back tomorrow?"**
> If yes, it belongs. If not, it probably doesn't.

---

## The journey

### First arrival
A visitor discovers the House. They **don't** immediately create an account. They're welcome to *wander* — enter the Great Hall, visit the Library, read, listen, explore. **The House asks nothing from them.** Eventually, on their own, they decide: *I'd like to stay.*

### Becoming a resident
The **Keeper** quietly appears — not dramatically, not as a popup. She simply asks:

> *"Would you like a room of your own?"*

Choosing to stay creates an account — not because accounts are required, but because **a room needs someone to belong to.**

### Receiving your key
Every resident receives a unique **House Key.** Not an NFT, not a badge — just *your key.* It quietly unlocks your **Room**, your **Journal**, your **Collection**, and **The House Remembers.** The key represents belonging, nothing more.

### Your Room
Every resident begins with the **same simple room**: a bed, a desk, a bookshelf, a window, a computer, a plant. Nothing extravagant. **The room becomes personal through living in the House — never through purchases.**

### Decorating (the House furnishes itself from your memories)
Rooms evolve naturally. Books you've kept appear on your shelves. Letters become stacks on your desk. Artwork hangs on your walls. Plants you've collected appear in pots. Seasonal decorations arrive on their own. **You don't decorate from a menu — the House slowly furnishes itself from your memories.**

### The House Remembers
The House quietly remembers — and **never announces it**: your first visit · your favourite room · the first book you opened · the first time Astro visited · your birthday · where you usually sit · what you tend to read. **Memory is quiet. Never gamified.**

---

## The neighbourhood

### The Hallways
Residents live along the **Hallways.** Each door belongs to someone. A door shows only **small clues** — current book, current playlist, light-on/away, a little seasonal decoration. Nothing else. **Curiosity drives exploration, not algorithms.**

### Visiting rooms
Visitors don't open profiles — **they knock.** If the room is open, they may enter, and see whatever the resident has chosen to share: bookshelf, desk, plants, music, artwork, collections, guest book, mood, seasonal decor.

### The Guest Book
Every room has one. Instead of comments, instead of likes — visitors leave short handwritten notes: *"Stopped by tonight." · "Borrowed your reading list." · "This room feels peaceful."* **Guest books become memories, not conversations.**

### Letters
Residents write one another **Letters.** Letters are private. When someone returns home, an **envelope waits on the desk** — not a notification. A letter.

---

## The residents of the House

### AI Luna
Lives in **Luna's Room.** She is *not* the House's librarian — she is **Luna's creative companion.** Residents may visit her room, knock, come inside, talk, create, brainstorm, and **leave inspired.**

### The Keeper
Belongs to the **House.** She rarely appears. She quietly recommends books, leaves notes, keeps your place, and protects the House's atmosphere. **She never interrupts, never advertises, never rushes.**

### Astro
Belongs to **everyone.** He has routines — sometimes he visits your room, sometimes he ignores you, sometimes he sleeps on your bed, sometimes he disappears for days. **His absence should feel meaningful.**

---

## MoonNet — the neighbourhood, not a network

MoonNet is **not social media.** It is the neighbourhood *inside* the House:

| Social media | MoonNet |
|---|---|
| Profiles | **Rooms** |
| Comments | **Guest Books** |
| DMs | **Letters** |
| Likes | **Collections** |
| Algorithms | **Discovery** |
| Performance | **Presence** |

---

## The one rule

The House asks only one thing of its residents: **honesty.** Not perfection. Not productivity. Not popularity. Just honesty.

## The success metric

The House succeeds when someone thinks *"I'll stop by for a few minutes"* — and quietly stays for an hour.

---

## 🌙 The First Night *(the ritual every resident experiences)*

The very first time someone decides to stay:

The House is dark. The Keeper hands them a key. She doesn't explain anything. She simply says:

> *"Your room is upstairs."*

The resident walks through the Hallways. Every door is closed. They find one with **their name engraved on a small brass plaque.** They unlock it.

The room is empty except for **a bed, a desk, a window, a candle, and a journal.**

On the desk is one handwritten note:

> ### *Welcome home.*
> ### *The rest of the room will learn who you are.*

They don't customise their room in a menu. **They live in it, and it slowly becomes theirs.**
