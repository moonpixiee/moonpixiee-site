## Render brief *(isolated — Layer 1 only)*

> Generate **one set of three Gothic-Victorian cathedral window frames with antique hand-blown glass** — a tall centre window and two slightly shorter side windows — viewed **straight on, centered, isolated on a fully transparent background.** Hand-carved **dark walnut** frames, not machine-perfect, with subtle botanical carvings, **hidden ravens, tiny crescents, and pressed-vine details**, and **delicate stained-glass inlays only around the borders** (crescent moon, ivy, a raven feather, tiny stars — like jewelry, never full stained glass). The **antique glass** is gently imperfect so light will bend through it. Lighting: **House Standard — cool moonlight key with a faint warm firelight glow on the inner face of the glass**, at rest. The glass reads as dark, empty, and ready to have a night sky placed behind it.
>
> **Absolutely NOT in this asset** *(each is its own layer):* no wall or stone the windows sit in · no sky, moon, stars, clouds, or landscape behind the glass · no candles, books, ivy, telescope, raven statue, flowers, or any sill props · no floor or ledge.
>
> **Design this as though someone will remember it twenty years from now.**
