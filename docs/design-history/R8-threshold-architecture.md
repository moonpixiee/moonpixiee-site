# R8 — Threshold Architectural Language

**Reference R8.** Committed 31 Jul 2026. Direction for the threshold's final form:
the architectural language the entire House inherits. See
`R8-threshold-architecture.webp`.

**Assignment:** the Threshold (exterior) — and, by inheritance, the material and
lighting grammar of the whole House.

**Direction (mood and architecture, NOT literal recreation):**
- A doorway carved into a **black stone / black-brick wall** with real thickness —
  heavy, old, weathered, touchable. The reveal smoothed by years of hands; the
  steps worn where feet have crossed.
- **The name is built into the architecture:** "House of Luna" as raised lettering
  with bevel and true shadow, catching the lantern, slightly imperfect — the
  Architect (Cormorant Garamond) in letter-spaced small caps per F-1. Architecture,
  not UI.
- **One lantern** above the entrance, warm amber; every shadow agrees with it.
  Nothing glows without reason.
- Materials: black masonry, volcanic stone, faint moisture, centuries.
- Atmosphere: **not** horror, haunted, or fantasy-game UI — welcoming and
  mysterious. An old library, candlelight, someone left the lamp on for you.
  Governing sentence: *"I've never been here before… but it feels like this place
  has been waiting for me."*

**Sigil — NOT adopted.** The crescent/star sigil above the name in the reference is
**omitted**. A "House sigil" is logged here as a **future proposal for deliberate
design** — not improvised into the threshold now.

**Spec guards (ratified law overrides the image):** the reference shows the greeting
on three lines; the House keeps the **ratified two-line break** ("…expecting" /
"you."). Copy and punctuation exactly per `PHASE-R-THRESHOLD-COPY.md`; protection
clause in force; text on quiet surfaces (contrast vs. the actual local surface);
complete still at 380px and in reduced-motion; performance budgets stand.

Origin: Luna's direction of 31 Jul 2026. The reference is treated as a mood board —
no image is copied; at least the lantern-lit stonework is interpreted through craft
+ the ratified palette, never lifted.
