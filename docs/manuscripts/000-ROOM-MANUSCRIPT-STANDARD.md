# 📖 The Room Manuscript — Standard
### House of Luna · the studio bible

> **Every room deserves its own manuscript before it deserves its own painting.**
> We are not building a website. We are building a studio. These manuscripts are the DNA — everything else grows from them. *(Ratified by Luna, 15 Aug 2026.)*

*Renamed from "Production Packet" to **Room Manuscript** — because this is the House, and every room is a chapter of it. Think Pixar's story bible, Imagineering's blue-sky packet, a game studio's environment bible: nobody paints anything until the room has been **written.***

---

## The one law

**No room gets made until it earns a manuscript.** This is how we protect the House from becoming generic — from becoming AI slop. The manuscript comes first, always. The image is an *interpretation of the truth*; the manuscript **is** the truth.

Corollary — **the manuscript never expires.** Five years from now you hire an illustrator: same manuscript. You build a VR version: same manuscript. You publish an art book, remake the site, license the world: **same manuscript.** The soul stays; only the medium changes.

---

## The pipeline *(nothing gets skipped, nothing gets rushed)*

```
💭 Idea
   ↓
📖 Room Manuscript      ← the soul is written (this document's job)
   ↓
🎬 Scene Direction      ← the manuscript's final chapter: a director's brief
   ↓
🖼 Art Direction        ← composition, palette, negative space for hotspots
   ↓
🎨 Final Environment    ← the render (an interpretation of the manuscript)
   ↓
⚡ Interactions          ← the invisible hotspot layer is laid over the set
   ↓
🏡 The room lives forever
```

*(The old "Room Art Bible" is not discarded — it is **absorbed**. Its craft — composition, lighting model, the negative-space-for-hotspots rule, the generation scaffold — now lives inside the manuscript's Architecture, Lighting, and Scene Direction chapters. One source of truth per room: the manuscript.)*

## The crew *(how we work together)*

- **Luna — the Imagineer.** The vision. *"I think the blanket should feel like someone just got up."*
- **Claude — the Creative Director.** Deepens every instinct into story. *"Okay — whose blanket is it? Why is it there? How old is it? What story does it tell?"*
- **Claude — the Technical Director.** Implements the answer into the set and the interactions. *"Perfect. I'll build it into the room."*

The Imagineer feels it, the Creative Director gives it a story, the Technical Director gives it a body. Nothing reaches the House without passing through all three.

## The chapters *(every manuscript has these, in this order)*

A manuscript opens with a **metadata block**, then these chapters. Not every chapter must be long — depth goes where the room needs it. Set Dressing is where we become obsessive.

1. **The Soul of the Room** — why it exists, *emotionally*. Not its function.
2. **The Story** — what happened here before we arrived. Who was here. Who comes here. What it has witnessed.
3. **The Visitor's Journey** — what someone feels at **5 seconds / 30 seconds / 5 minutes**, and what makes them stay.
4. **The Emotional Palette** — what the room should feel like, and (just as important) what it must **never** feel like.
5. **Architecture** — everything physical, like an architect: ceiling, materials, windows, fireplaces, shelves, doors, hidden passages, furniture.
6. **Set Dressing** — *every object, one by one.* Age, material, wear, colour, temperature, smell, history, and **why it is HERE.** This is the obsessive chapter.
7. **The Cast** — the Keeper's traces and Astro: presence by evidence, never as a feature.
8. **Sound** — no music. Just the room: fire, rain, clock, floorboards, wind, a distant page.
9. **Lighting** — where **every** light comes from and where **every** shadow falls. Across morning / night / rain / winter / moonlight.
10. **Discovery Map** — every hotspot, every curiosity, every future interaction — even ones we won't build for five years.
11. **Hidden Stories** — what only observant people find. The brass key. The pressed fern. The scratched chair. The carved moon.
12. **Things We Refuse To Do** — the room's negative constraints (no glowing hotspots, no UI, no clutter, no object without a story).
13. **The Final Test** — the single bar the finished room must clear.
14. **The House Whispers** — the one thing the room would say if it could speak.
15. **Scene Direction** — *and only now* do we describe the image. **Not a prompt — a director's brief.** This is what goes to the artist (or the AI). "Paint the room described in the previous chapters." **Every Scene Direction ends with the same line — always:**
    > *"Design this as though someone will remember it twenty years from now."*
    That line isn't for the AI. It's for **us** — the reminder that every set piece must *earn* its place in the House.
16. **Legacy** — the closing tradition (below).

---

## The two traditions *(sacred — every manuscript ends the same way)*

**The Legacy page.** One question, answered honestly:
> *If someone remembers only ONE thing about this room twenty years from now — what do we hope it is?*

It is not the prettiest thing, or the most impressive thing. It is the **true** thing. *(For the Fireplace, the answer is: "It felt like the safest place on the internet." If we achieve that, everything else becomes easier.)*

**The ratification line.** Every manuscript closes with the same words, and its stamp:
> **This room now belongs to the House.**
>
> *Room Manuscript #NNN · Ratified: [date] · Status: Canon*

That line matters because it means we are no longer *inventing* these places. We are **discovering** them — and once discovered, they belong to the House, and every future expansion must respect their truth. **The House bends around its history; its history does not bend around new features.**

## Version control *(keep Version 1 forever)*

- Every version is an **immutable file**: `001-the-library-fireplace-v1.0.md`. A change makes a **new** file (`v1.1`, `v2.0`) — we never overwrite, never delete. Ten years from now the folder still holds `Library Fireplace v1.0 (2026)`, the very first, exactly as written.
- The **soul is versioned like software; the truth of the room does not change** — it only deepens. *(v8.3, "Expanded for VR": new medium, same soul.)*
- These files become part of House of Luna's own history. One day they are seven leather-bound books on a shelf — *the actual manuscripts that built the House.* That is how a project becomes a legacy.

---

## Manuscripts & Chapters *(the two scales)*

A small room is a **Manuscript** of its own. A large space — the Library, a whole wing — is a **Manuscript composed of Chapters**, one per room or experience inside it. Same rigor per chapter; together the chapters read like a book. *The Library is a Manuscript; the Fireplace is its **Chapter I.*** We don't build "a Library" — we build ten small, unforgettable experiences that, together, become the Library. Nothing gets skipped; every chapter gets the same love the Fireplace got.
