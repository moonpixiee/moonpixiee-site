## I. The Soul of the Door

The Library Door is not a passage. It is a **promise.** You are still standing in the Grand Hall when you meet it — and in that instant, before you've stepped anywhere, something quietly lands: *this place is real.* The Door's entire job is that realization. It doesn't show you the Library. **It makes you want it.**

## II. The Door's Laws *(House Law — do not casually change)*

1. **The door is ajar** — never fully open, never fully closed. Open shows too much; closed refuses; **ajar is the whole feeling of the House.**
2. **Warm orange firelight spills out** of the gap, across the cool stone of the Grand Hall floor — a path of light laid at your feet.
3. **Visitors don't see the Library — they glimpse it.** A sliver of book-spines, a suggestion of depth, the glow of a fire you can't yet see. *(LAW 008.)*
4. **Entering feels like crossing a threshold, not loading a webpage.** *(LAW 009 — the transition is a walk.)*
5. **The memory of this room is:** *"I remember realizing this place was real."*

## III. The Moment — *the first few seconds*

You're in the Grand Hall. Ahead, a great old door stands **ajar,** and **warm orange light** leaks from the gap and lies in a long soft rectangle across the floor. You **hear** a fire, faint, somewhere beyond. The smell of old paper, implied. Through the sliver you catch only the edge of things — a few spines, a warm glow, dark wood climbing out of sight. No one is visible. Nothing asks anything of you.

And the thought arrives on its own: *"Wait… this actually exists."* The feeling of finding something on the internet that felt impossible — old Tumblr, old DeviantArt, a place that shouldn't be able to exist and somehow does.

## IV. Set Dressing *(of the Door — only the Door)*

- **The Door.** Tall, heavy, ancient, hand-worn walnut and iron; standing ajar at an angle that hides the room and reveals the light.
- **The Spill of Light.** Warm orange-gold firelight across the Grand Hall's cooler stone — the single most important element in frame. It is the lure.
- **The Glimpse.** In the gap: a sliver of book-spines, the faint suggestion of a fire's glow, depth falling away into warm dark. Never enough to *see* the Library — only enough to want it.
- **A candle** at the doorway, steady. Maybe, just inside the gap, the edge of a coat on a hook — barely caught.
- **The seam.** This is where the Grand Hall's *illustrated* fidelity gives way to the Library's *immersive* fidelity. The Door is the border between the two worlds. *(Production note: fidelity deepens as you go inward — the Hall is iconic illustration; the Library is immersive; the Door is the crossing. Different fidelity per depth is intentional and good.)*

## V. Lighting

The whole frame lives on **one contrast**: the **cool** light of the Grand Hall vs. the **warm orange** spilling from the gap (Law 003 — Moonlight cool, Firelight warm). The warm light is not decoration; it is **direction** — it tells the eye, and the feet, where to go. Comfortable darkness everywhere else (Law 005).

## VI. Sound *(Law 009 — hear the next room before you see it)*

Through the gap: a fire, faint. A single page turning, far off. The Grand Hall's own hush around you. When you cross, the fire grows *slightly* louder before the Library ever appears — you arrive by ear first.

## VII. Scene Direction
*A director's brief for the render — the Library Door as seen from within the Grand Hall. Paint only the Door.*

Night, inside the Grand Hall. Ahead of us, set into the Hall's architecture, a **great old door stands ajar** — heavy dark walnut and iron, worn by centuries. From the gap, **warm orange firelight spills out** and lies in a long soft rectangle across the Hall's cool stone floor, reaching toward us. Through the narrow opening we glimpse only fragments: the edge of tall book-spines, the faint glow of an unseen fire, dark shelving climbing up out of view — **never the room itself, only the promise of it.** A steady candle by the doorframe. The Grand Hall around the door stays cooler, quieter, rendered in its iconic fantasy-illustration key; the warm light from the gap is the one bright, living thing, and every line leads the eye toward it.

The feeling: **the moment a website becomes a world** — *someone left it open for you, and you suddenly realize this place is real.* **Not in the plate:** no people (the Keeper's welcome is a later layer), no cat, no text/UI/labels. Cool Hall + warm spilling firelight only; ~5% parallax bleed past every edge; leave the Hall floor calm so the light-path reads. Photoreal at the door's edge softening into the Hall's illustrative style — the seam between fidelities is intentional. 16:9, volumetric warm light, natural grain, no lens flare.

## VIII. Things We Refuse To Do

No fully-open door (reveals too much). No fully-closed door (refuses). No signage, no "Enter" prompt, no button, no page-load cut. Nothing that shows the whole Library at once. Nothing that turns *arriving* into *clicking.*

## IX. The Final Test

Someone clicks "The Library" expecting a page. If, for one held second, they instead feel *"…oh. This is real. I want to go in"* — the Door has done its entire job.
