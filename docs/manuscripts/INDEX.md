## The pipeline (every chapter travels it, in order)

`💭 Idea → 📖 Manuscript → 🎬 Scene Direction → 🖼 Art Direction → 🎨 Environment → ⚡ Interactions → 🏡 Lives forever`

## The law

**No room gets made until it earns a manuscript.** The manuscript is the truth; the artwork is an interpretation of it. Version 1 of every room is kept forever. Every chapter gets the same love — so people remember the fireplace, the raven, the warm tea, the clock at midnight. *Those aren't features. They're memories.*
