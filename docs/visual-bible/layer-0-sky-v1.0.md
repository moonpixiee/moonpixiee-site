# 🌙 Visual Bible — Layer 0 · The Sky
### House of Luna · The Library · Status: **Canon v1.0**

> *"Outside, the night stretches endlessly. Inside, the House keeps the light."*

This is not a prompt. It is a **creative contract.** An illustrator, a 3D artist, an AI, or a whole game studio could each read it and arrive at the same emotional destination, even if their artwork differs. *Paint this.*

---

## Purpose

The sky is **not** the focus of the composition. It exists to quietly remind every visitor that the House is part of a much larger world. It should never compete with the architecture. It breathes gently through the cathedral windows, making the Library feel timeless, lived-in, and alive.

**The sky is scenery. The House is the protagonist.**

## Emotional goal

The visitor should never think *"That's a beautiful sky."* They should feel: **"The world outside has gone quiet."** The sky creates *permission to slow down.*

## Governing Law

**LAW 001 — The House Exists Outside Time.** The sky is permanently night. Morning, afternoon, and sunset do not exist. It is always the hour when the rest of the world has gone to sleep. *(See also LAW 002 The Night Is Alive, LAW 003 Moonlight Is Sacred, LAW 006 The Moon Keeps Its Own Calendar.)*

## Composition

Visible **only** through the Library's towering Gothic windows. The sky occupies roughly **15–20%** of the visible composition; the Library always remains visually dominant. The visitor never feels they are looking at an outdoor scene — they are **safely inside, looking outward.**

## Color palette

Never pure black. Layered, natural night tones — **midnight blue · deep indigo · charcoal · muted navy · soft violet undertones** — transitioning smoothly, without obvious banding. *Almost black, but still full of colour.*

## The Moon

Ancient, patient, **not oversized, not fantasy.** A real moon in soft **silver** — never glowing blue, never cartoonish. Large enough to be noticed, never filling the window; distant. Its light gently illuminates portions of the room. It **occasionally slips behind passing clouds,** subtly changing the brightness inside the Library.

## Stars

**Sparse.** Only the brightest are visible — some almost invisible. No constellations emphasized, no Milky Way, no magical glowing particles. Believable. If someone watches long enough, one might **twinkle.**

## Clouds

Move **almost imperceptibly** — a full crossing of the moon takes several minutes. Soft wisps, never storm clouds. **Visitors notice the changing light before they notice the clouds themselves.** The motion is meditative.

## Weather

Default is **calm** — no rain, no snow, no wind. Weather becomes meaningful *precisely because it is uncommon.* When it appears, it should feel like the House has quietly **changed its mood.**

## Wind

Never directly visible. Implied only — slow cloud movement, a distant branch, a subtle shift in moonlight. The House remains protected from the elements.

## Audio relationship

The sky produces **no sound.** Silence belongs to the night. All ambience originates from *within* the Library. The outside world remains respectfully distant.

## Animation

Nothing loops noticeably. Movement is so subtle that visitors only recognize it after several minutes. **The sky behaves like a living oil painting, not a video.**

## Distance & the horizon

**The horizon is never visible** — the castle sits too high, or too deep. Only fragments of the world beyond can be seen: silhouettes of ancient trees, distant rooftops, an occasional mountain outline. **The House never reveals everything.** Mystery remains part of the landscape.

## Visual priority *(the eye's path)*

1. The Fireplace → 2. The Bookshelves → 3. The Chair → 4. The Windows → **5. The Sky.**
The sky always *supports* the room; it never dominates it.

## The rare grace — a shooting star

Once in a very long while — maybe once every few weeks for a regular visitor — a **shooting star.** It triggers nothing: no achievement, no sound, no confetti. Just… *blink… gone.* The kind of thing someone posts about: *"Guys… did anyone else just see a shooting star from the Library window, or am I losing it?"* Quiet magic that makes the House feel real.

## Things we refuse to do

No sunsets · no daytime · no bright blue skies · no oversized fantasy moons · no auroras · no galaxies · no exaggerated lightning · no animated background that distracts from reading.

## Final test

If the visitor spends ten minutes reading by the fire, the sky should quietly convince them **time has stopped.** They should not remember *looking* at the sky. They should remember **how the room made them feel.**

## The Memory *(Prime Law closing)*

> *"I remember looking up from my book and realizing the moon had slipped behind the clouds."*
