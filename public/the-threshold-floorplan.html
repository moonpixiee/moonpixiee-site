You are standing at the Threshold. You cannot see the whole Library — but you can sense it. Each room reaches toward you with a different sense, and your own eyes, ears, and nose decide where you wander. This is not a map you're given. It's a room you feel.

LAW 012 — The House Is One Connected Place.
No room is an island. From anywhere in the House you can sense its neighbours — a glow, a crackle, rain on far glass, music from above, the smell of tea. You always feel the next room before you enter it. That is the difference between a collection of beautiful images and one living place.
"You can hear the kitchens. You glimpse the staircases. The castle feels connected." — that's what we're building.
